How to play

  • Drag from the launcher (bottom center) to set aim and power.
  • Release to shoot the ball into the field.
  • Hit bumpers and targets to score. Your ball drains if it falls out the bottom.
  • After your ball drains, the computer takes its shot.
  • After 3 balls each, highest total score wins.
